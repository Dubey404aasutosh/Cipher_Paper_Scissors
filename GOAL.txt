Objective: Design a hybrid encryption algorithm by combining the concepts of Caesar Cipher and Playfair Cipher along with any additional encryption logic of your choice.

Instructions
1. Work in groups of 2–4 students.
2. Design a unique cipher which should mandatorily use the concepts of both Caesar Cipher and Playfair Cipher.
3. Give your cipher a unique name.
4. Write the encryption algorithm step by step.
5. Write the decryption algorithm that correctly recovers the original plaintext.
6. Demonstrate your algorithm using one sample plaintext and a key and show:

* Plaintext
* Ciphertext
* Decrypted Plaintext

7. Briefly explain why your cipher is stronger than using Caesar Cipher alone.

8. Implement your algorithm in C programming (in OnlineGDB).
9. Submit a single PDF (in Google Classroom) containing:

* Group Members
* Cipher Name
* Encryption Algorithm
* Decryption Algorithm
* Example with Encryption & Decryption
* Brief Security Explanation

Note: Creativity is encouraged, but your encryption and decryption processes must be logically correct and reversible.
