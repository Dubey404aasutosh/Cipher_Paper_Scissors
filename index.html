The Three Moves

How our hybrid cipher layers three distinct cryptographic primitives to eliminate frequency analysis and structure.

01

Shift (Caesar)

C₁ = (P + k₁) mod 26

Rotates every character forward in the alphabet by a secret integer key. Pre-whitens text before grid encoding.

02

Swap (Playfair)

5x5 Grid Substitution

Pairs letters into digraphs and substitutes them using a dynamic key-generated 5x5 matrix for polygraphic defense.

03

Flip (Letter XOR)

Bitwise XOR in [A-Z]

Applies 5-bit XOR between letter indices and key stream, mapped losslessly back onto the A-Z alphabet domain.
