Why CPS-26 Beats Caesar Alone

Single-primitive classical ciphers are easily broken with modern compute. Cipher, Paper, Scissors introduces multi-layered confusion & diffusion:

  • Eliminates Single-Letter Frequencies: Pre-shifting with Caesar breaks raw digraph structures before Playfair grid substitution.
  • Polygraphic Digraph Shield: Substituting pairs via 5x5 key matrix destroys monographic character probability distributions.
  • Bitwise XOR Diffusion: The final Letter-Domain XOR layer ensures identical plaintext letters produce completely different ciphertext outputs.
  • Zero Distortion Reversibility: Custom modular arithmetic maintains 100% exact text recovery upon decryption.
